About ExamPrep

We help students revise less, and remember more.

ExamPrep is a small, independent site built around one belief: most students work hard enough. What they are missing is a method.

What we publish

Two detailed guides and three planners. The guides explain techniques that have been tested in classrooms and laboratories for decades. The planners turn those techniques into a timetable you can follow tomorrow morning.

How we check it

Each guide is drafted from teaching and tutoring experience, compared with published research on learning and memory, and reviewed by a second reader. We describe evidence in plain words and say clearly when something is only our opinion.

What we are not

We are not a school, an exam board or a tutoring service, and we cannot mark work or predict grades. No planner can promise a result. What a good plan can do is make sure your effort goes where it counts.

ExamPrep is based at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. Questions and corrections are welcome on the contact page.
